Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about acquisition, delivery, and manufacture warranties.

How does ChronoLux verify watch authenticity and calibration?

Every ChronoLux timepiece is accompanied by a cryptographically serialized NFC Certificate of Authenticity, physical chronometer certificate (-2/+2 sec COSC standard), and Geneva inspection dossier signed by our Head of Horology.

What does the 2-Year International Warranty cover?

Our warranty covers all internal mechanical components, escapement timing regulation, water resistance seals, and hands/indices alignment. Registering your timepiece in our Atelier Vault extends your coverage to 5 full years with complimentary first service.

How is the Armored White-Glove delivery handled?

All shipments travel via secure armored courier with full transit insurance. A biometric or signed handover is coordinated in advance by your dedicated VIP concierge at your private residence or private bank.

Can I schedule a private viewing at a ChronoLux boutique?

Yes. We welcome private appointments at our salons in Geneva, New York 5th Avenue, London Mayfair, Tokyo Ginza, and Dubai Mall. You may book your appointment below or through our VIP Concierge line.
